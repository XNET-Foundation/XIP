# XIP-14: Fractals Receivables Financing for XNET

**Authors:** Fractals
**Status:** In Development  
**Type:** Ecosystem / Operator Financing  
**Created:** 2026-07-02

## Abstract

Fractals is developing a receivables financing program to help accelerate cash flow for XNET operators. The program gives operators the option to receive accelerated payments against earned receivables, rather than waiting for the normal carrier reconciliation and settlement cycle.

## Motivation

XNET earns carrier offload revenue as traffic is generated across the network, but those payments are typically received after the carrier reconciliation and settlement cycle. In practice, this can mean operators wait 60–90 days before receiving cash for revenue generated today.

XNET operators deploy and maintain real-world telecom infrastructure. That requires upfront capital, ongoing maintenance, and the ability to reinvest as the network grows. Waiting months for earned revenue can slow down expansion.

Fractals aims to solve this timing gap by offering operators the option to receive accelerated payments against earned receivables. By factoring XNET receivables, Fractals can help operators access cash sooner, improve working capital, and reinvest faster into their XNET deployments.

## How It Works

Operators who participate may choose to receive a faster payout in exchange for a financing fee. Operators who do not participate can continue receiving payments on the normal settlement schedule.

At a high level:

1. Carrier offload traffic is generated across the XNET network.
2. Revenue is reported and reconciled through existing systems.
3. Operator allocations are calculated.
4. Participating operators can receive an accelerated payment from Fractals.
5. When the carrier payment is later received, the financed receivable is reconciled.

The goal is not to change XNET's core economics or carrier relationships. The goal is simply to give operators more flexibility around when they receive their earned revenue.

## Summary

Fractals receivables financing gives XNET operators a simple choice: wait for the normal carrier settlement cycle, or receive accelerated payment on eligible earned revenue.

The purpose is straightforward: improve operator liquidity, reduce the friction created by delayed settlement cycles, and help XNET infrastructure grow faster.
